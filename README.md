## 📌 Problem Statement
Engineering students frequently balance disparate study assets: 60-minute recorded lecture videos, multi-hundred-page textbooks, and fragmented slide decks. Traditional conversational AI assistants suffer from frequent **hallucinations** and **lack verifiable source provenance**. When a student asks a conceptual question, conventional tools cannot cite the exact slide page or video timestamp that validates the answer.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview
**OmniTutor AI** serves as an adaptive multimodal study companion that unifies slides, textbooks, and video lectures into a grounded, source-cited knowledge base. 

### Key Capabilities:
- **Spatial & Temporal Grounding:** Automatically extracts document pages from lecture PDFs and millisecond-level timestamps from video transcripts.
- **Strict Evidence Citation:** In-text references anchor every factual premise directly to physical evidence (e.g., `[Slide/PDF Pg. 2]` or `[Lecture Video 02:15]`).
- **Adaptive Diagnostic Assessment:** Synthesizes conceptual diagnostic quizzes based on course materials and serves targeted remedial hints if the student answers incorrectly.
